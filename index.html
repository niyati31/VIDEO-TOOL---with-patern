How the mark sizing works

Adjacent cells with similar colours are grouped into 1, 4, 9, 16 or more-cell blocks. A 1-cell group uses a 30 px mark; 4 cells use 60 px; 9 cells use 90 px, up to 150 px. Groups are capped at 25 cells and the shape scales to fit the grouped area.
